Before we start.

Simple answers about how we work.

Do you only work in Tunis?

Remote support is available throughout Tunisia. On-site visits are organized according to location and the nature of the need.

Yes. We support your current platform or help you choose the one that best fits your organization.

Can you take over an existing IT environment?

Yes. We first assess the environment and then propose a progressive transition with priorities, documentation and security improvements.

Are plans priced per user?

The quote can account for users, devices, sites and service level. The exact model is defined after the assessment.
